The small print, made simple

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Here’s how the proposed Relay plans work. This is a fictional product concept, not a live offer.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are paid upfront for 12 months. The monthly rate shown is the annual total divided by 12: Starter is $120, Team is $264, and Scale is $480 per member per year. Monthly plans are paid one month at a time. All prices are in USD, before applicable taxes.

Who counts as a paid member?

Each person with access to your workspace counts as one paid member, including admins. Every plan starts at one member, with no minimum seat commitment beyond that. Automation limits apply to the entire workspace; storage allowances are pooled across paid members.

Can I change my plan or cancel?

In this proposed model, upgrades and added seats are prorated for the remainder of your billing term. Downgrades, seat reductions, and cancellations take effect at the end of that term. You keep access until then; unused time is not refunded. This demo cannot start or cancel a subscription.

What happens if we reach a usage limit?

There are no automatic overage charges. At the project or storage limit, you can remove or archive work, or choose a larger plan. Automations pause when the workspace’s monthly allowance is used and resume when it resets. An upgrade would always require your confirmation.

Can I try Relay from this page?

This is a self-contained pricing demo, so there’s no live product, sign-up, or trial. Choose any plan to calculate a local estimate for your team. No payment details are requested, and nothing is submitted or stored.
