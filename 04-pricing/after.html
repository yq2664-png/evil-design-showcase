Questions, answered plainly

How does per-member billing work?

Each active member on your workspace counts as one seat. You can add or remove members at any time, and the next invoice reflects the change.

What happens if I switch from monthly to annual?

Annual billing charges once for twelve months at the discounted per-member rate. Any unused monthly time is credited toward the annual charge.

Can I cancel or downgrade later?

Yes. You can cancel or move to a lower plan from workspace settings. Paid plans stay active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Do you offer anything for nonprofits or education?

We do. Contact our team with a little about your organization and we will share the current options.
