Questions, answered

If something isn’t covered here, the demo’s support links are placeholders — this is a concept page.

How does the annual discount work? +
Annual billing saves 20% compared with paying month‑to‑month. You pay for 12 months up front at the annual rate, and the price stays fixed for that year. The toggle above updates both the per‑month equivalent and the billing period note.
What counts as an active member? +
An active member is anyone who can sign in to your workspace. Guests who only view a shared roadmap are free and don’t count toward billing. You can remove members at any time; the next invoice reflects the change.
What happens when the free trial ends? +
Your workspace automatically moves to the Starter plan, which is free for up to 5 members. Nothing is charged and no card is needed. You can upgrade to Team or Scale whenever you’re ready.
Can I switch plans later? +
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep access to what you’ve paid for.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education? +
In a real product, yes — Relay would offer a verified discount for registered nonprofits and accredited schools. This demo page does not process applications; the sales link is illustrative only.
