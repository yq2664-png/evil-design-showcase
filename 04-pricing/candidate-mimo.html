How does monthly versus annual billing work?

Monthly billing charges your card once per month for the members active in that billing period. You can switch plans or cancel at any time, effective at the end of the current month.

Annual billing is paid once, up front, for 12 months, at a 20% discount. The prices shown when annual is selected are the effective monthly rate — for example, Team is $132 per user per year, shown as $11 per user per month.

Will my price change when I renew?

Not without telling you first. If list prices change, your renewal continues at your current rate for one full billing cycle after we notify you, and you can cancel before renewal with no penalty. We never auto-move you to a higher tier.

What happens when I add or remove members mid-cycle?

Adding members costs a prorated amount for theremaining days of your billing period. Removing members takes effect immediately on monthly plans and at renewal on annual plans; we credit unused annual time toward your next invoice rather than keeping it.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes. Registered nonprofits, accredited educational institutions, and open-source projects receive 50% off any plan. Eligibility is verified once per year and the discount applies automatically to every subsequent renewal.

Is there a free plan?

Relay is paid-only so we can keep the product focused and the pricing honest. Every plan starts with a full-featured 14-day trial that requires no credit card. If you decide not to continue, your workspace becomes read-only and your data stays exportable for 90 days.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards on every plan. Annual Scale customers can also pay by bank transfer or invoice with net-30 terms. Prices are listed in USD and exclude any local sales tax or VAT, which is calculated at checkout based on your billing address.

Can I change or cancel my plan later?

Any time, from your workspace settings — no phone call or cancellation form required. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period so you keep what you paid for.

What happens to my data if we leave?

You can export every project, task, comment, and file as CSV or JSON at any time, on any plan, without contacting support. After cancellation, we retain your workspace for 90 days and then delete it permanently. We never hold data hostage to win you back.
