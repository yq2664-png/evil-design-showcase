Why Forma

Fewer, better things.

We design around three simple principles. Everything we make has to earn its place in your home — and stay there for a very long time.

Materials with a past

Recycled stoneware, reclaimed oak and GOTS-certified cotton. Every material is traceable to its source and chosen for how gracefully it ages.

Made in small batches

We produce in runs of 200 or fewer with a workshop we've partnered with since day one — less waste, fairer wages, and no surplus stock heading to landfill.

Materials & making

Honest materials, measured impact.

We publish the footprint of every product we make — the clay, the glaze, the freight and the packaging. Not because it looks good on a page, but because you can't improve what you don't count.

Our workshop in Stoke-on-Trent fires with renewable electricity, reclaims its process water, and turns trimmings back into usable clay. Nothing is wasted, and nothing is hidden.

  • ✓ Plastic-free packaging — moulded recycled paper, paper tape, zero filler.
  • ✓ Water-based, lead-free glazes tested to food-contact standards.
  • ✓ Carbon-measured delivery with 1% of revenue to verified restoration projects.

Talk to a real person

Three things: we use recycled or renewable materials wherever possible, we produce in small batches so nothing is over-made, and we guarantee every piece for ten years with free repairs. We also publish a lifecycle footprint for each product so you can see the numbers behind the claims.

How does the ten-year repair guarantee work? +

If a piece chips, cracks or fails through normal use within ten years, send it back to us and we'll repair it free of charge, including return postage. If it genuinely can't be repaired, we'll replace it and reclaim the original materials for reuse.

Moulded recycled paper pulp, paper tape and recycled card — no plastic, no polystyrene, no foam. Everything is kerbside recyclable, and the outer box is made from 100% post-consumer waste.

We ship across the UK and EU, with dispatch within 48 hours on working days. UK delivery is typically 2–3 days, EU delivery 4–7 days. Orders over £80 ship free, and all delivery emissions are measured and offset.
