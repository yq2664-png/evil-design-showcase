Why Forma

Beautiful by nature. Useful by design.

Every piece is developed with independent makers, using materials that can return to the earth or be repaired, refilled, and loved again.

01

Earth-first materials

Responsibly sourced clay, reclaimed timber, and natural fibres. No virgin plastics, no unnecessary finishes.

02

Made to be mended

Simple forms and honest construction mean our pieces age gracefully — and can be repaired rather than replaced.

03

Small-batch by choice

We produce in considered runs with maker partners we know by name, keeping waste low and quality high.
