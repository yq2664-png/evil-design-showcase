A little good to know.

A few thoughtful habits can give everyday favourites a longer life.

How do I care for my ceramics?

For these concept pieces, we recommend gentle handwashing with mild soap and a soft cloth. Avoid sudden temperature changes and let each piece dry fully before putting it away.

What keeps linen looking its best?

Wash gently at a cool temperature with similar colours. Air-dry when you can, and embrace the creases — linen’s relaxed texture is part of its charm.

Can I place a real order?

Forma is a fictional brand and this is an interactive concept store. You can explore the collection and build a sample bag, but there are no payments, shipments or real orders.
