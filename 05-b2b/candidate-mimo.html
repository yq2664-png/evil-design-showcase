The operational gap

Your systems record the work.
They don't run it.

Most distribution networks already have the data they need — it's just spread across a WMS, a TMS, spreadsheets, radio calls and the experience of one or two supervisors who know where everything goes wrong.

The result is a shift that starts with good intentions and ends in firefighting. Decisions are made on stale numbers, exceptions surface too late to fix, and every site runs its own version of "the process".

“We weren't short of reports. We were short of the ten minutes between finding a problem and being able to do something about it.”

— Operations Director, national 3PL network
01 · Fragmented

Nine sources of truth

WMS, LMS, yard, automation and ERP each hold part of the picture. Reconciling them is manual work that happens after the shift has already moved on.

02 · Reactive

Exceptions found too late

Short picks, late trailers and labour gaps surface at handover — when the cost of correcting them has already been paid in overtime and missed cut-offs.

03 · Inconsistent

Every site runs differently

Best practice lives with individuals. Rolling out a process change across twelve facilities takes months and drifts within weeks.

04 · Unmeasured

Improvement can't be proven

Without a shared baseline, initiatives are judged on anecdotes. Boards see cost per unit; operators can't see what changed it.

How AtlasFlow works

One operational loop, repeated every shift.

AtlasFlow sits above the systems you already run. It unifies their signals, turns them into a plan your teams can execute, and closes the loop with measurable results.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Pre-built connectors pull orders, inventory, labour, yard and automation telemetry into a single operational model.

    • Read-only or two-way sync
    • Historical backfill included
    • No WMS replacement
  2. 2

    Plan

    Demand, labour and capacity are modelled into a shift plan with clear targets, staffing and wave sequencing.

    • Scenario planning before commit
    • Constraint-aware scheduling
    • Site and network views
  3. 3

    Direct

    Work is dispatched to supervisors and associates with live priorities, and exceptions are escalated the moment thresholds break.

    • Role-based work queues
    • Threshold-based alerting
    • Mobile and wallboard views
  4. 4

    Improve

    Every shift is scored against plan, so root causes are visible and process changes can be proven across sites.

    • Plan-vs-actual analytics
    • Root-cause tagging
    • Standardised playbooks

Common questions

Answers before the first call.

Do we have to replace our WMS?

No. AtlasFlow is deliberately not a system of record. It reads from and, where useful, writes back to the systems you already run. Most customers keep their WMS, ERP and labour platforms exactly as they are and use AtlasFlow to coordinate decisions across them.

How long does implementation really take?

A single-site pilot with two to four integrations typically reaches first measured value in six to ten weeks. Multi-site rollouts follow the pilot and are paced to your change capacity rather than a fixed calendar.

What does AtlasFlow need from our data?

Order and inventory events, labour or task records, and a schedule of cut-offs and constraints. Automation and yard telemetry are optional but improve exception detection. We backfill historical data where available so your baseline is real from day one.

How do supervisors actually adopt it?

AtlasFlow replaces the spreadsheet and whiteboard layer rather than adding another screen to check. Supervisors get one prioritised queue per shift, and configuration is done with them during the pilot so the thresholds match how their building really behaves.

How is AtlasFlow priced?

Annual subscription based on connected sites and operational volume tiers, with unlimited users. Implementation is scoped separately and fixed before kickoff. We share indicative pricing in the first conversation so there are no surprises later.

What happens if a connector fails?

Integrations are monitored continuously with automatic retry, dead-letter queues and alerting to both your team and ours. Data freshness is displayed in the product so operators always know how current their view is.
