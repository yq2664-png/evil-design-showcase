The problem

Operational signals arrive late, separate, and already stale.

Most warehouse teams do not lack data. They lack a shared, timely view of what the data means for the next hour of work.

01

Fragmented systems

WMS, labour tools and spreadsheets each hold part of the story. Directors reconcile them by hand.

02
