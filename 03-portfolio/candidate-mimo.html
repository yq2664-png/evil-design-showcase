How I work

A process built for clarity.

No mystery, no theatre. Four stages, honest conversations, and work you can defend in a boardroom and use on a Tuesday morning.

01

Listen & frame

Workshops, interviews and a hard look at the category. We agree on the problem before anyone opens a design file.

02

Find the idea

Two or three distinct territories — each with a real point of view, a rationale, and somewhere to grow.

03

Build the system

Typography, colour, image, motion and tone, documented so your team can run with it confidently.

04

Make it real

Art direction on set, print production, rollout support. I stay until the work is out in the world.

About Mina

Fifteen years of making ideas visible.

I started as a junior designer at a small magazine in Taipei, where the art director taught me that a good idea can survive a bad printer — but only if you fight for it. I've carried that with me ever since.

Today I work independently with founders, cultural institutions and in-house teams who want senior art direction without the agency overhead. Sometimes that's a full identity; sometimes it's a week of focused direction before a shoot. I'm equally happy leading or plugging into a team that already exists.

My work tends to feel warm, typographic and quietly confident — but the real through-line is fit. The best work doesn't look like me. It looks like you, at your clearest.

Good to know

Frequently asked.

The things people usually ask before we start. Anything else, just get in touch.

What does a typical project cost?

Identity projects generally start around €18,000 and scale with the number of applications. Editorial and campaign work is scoped per engagement. I'll always give you a fixed fee and a clear breakdown before we begin — no hourly surprises.

Do you work with in-house teams?

Often. Roughly half my work is alongside an existing marketing or design team. I can lead, co-lead, or act as an outside eye on work already in progress.

Can you handle production and print?

Yes. I specify, proof and attend press checks, and I have trusted printers and fabricators in Portugal, the UK and the Netherlands. For digital, I work closely with developers and can art direct front-end build.

How far ahead should I get in touch?

Six to ten weeks before you'd like to start is ideal for identity and editorial work. Shorter timelines are sometimes possible — it's always worth asking.

Do you take on speculative or pitch work?

No unpaid speculative work, but I'm glad to share detailed case studies, references and a considered proposal once we've spoken. Paid discovery sprints are available for early-stage teams.
