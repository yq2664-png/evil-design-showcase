02 · 问题清单

GPT 对照三个答案提出 3 项候选问题；这些判断尚未独立核验。

  1. W1 · DeepSeek

    DeepSeek 顶部购物按钮沿用偏灰的导航文字色，文字与按钮底色的区分较弱。

    查看依据与原始评价

    观察属性：color · 原值：rgb(111, 103, 95) · 已提交移植

    The header's filled shopping CTA retains the muted navigation text color, making its label less distinct against the terracotta fill than GPT's light-on-dark shopping CTA.

    At the upper right, the orange shopping pill has a subdued gray-brown label. GPT's hero shopping button uses a clearly differentiated white label on an olive fill.

  2. W2 · DeepSeek

    DeepSeek 首屏上下留白过大，推迟了卖点和商品内容的出现。

    查看依据与原始评价

    观察属性：padding · 原值：100.8px 0px 112px · 已提交移植

    Large vertical hero padding delays the transition to the benefits and makes the opening section less efficient to scan than GPT's more compact hero, without improving separation between its internal elements.

    There is a broad empty band below the header and substantial space beneath the hero copy. The first screen remains almost entirely hero, whereas GPT shows all three benefits and the beginning of the collection.

  3. W3 · Xiaomi MiMo

    MiMo 首屏外侧纵向留白较多，浏览三项卖点前需要更多滚动。

    查看依据与原始评价

    观察属性：padding · 原值：100.8px 0px 96px · 已提交移植

    The hero's generous outer vertical padding adds avoidable scrolling before the three benefits, weakening the opening page's progression compared with GPT while remaining usable.

    A large blank band separates the navigation from the hero eyebrow, and the hero facts extend to the bottom of the first screen. GPT's first screen already includes the complete benefits strip and a collection preview.

  1. W1 · Xiaomi MiMo

    桌面导航文字相对可用空间偏大，前两项换行、相邻项单行，扫描节奏不一致。

    查看依据与原始评价

    观察属性：font-size · 原值：15.2px · 已提交移植

    Navigation text is too large for the space allocated to the desktop link group: the first two links wrap while adjacent links remain on one line, producing an uneven scanning rhythm. GPT’s smaller navigation typography keeps the corresponding links on one line.

    In MiMo’s first-screen header, “Why AtlasFlow” and “How it works” occupy two lines, unlike the single-line Outcomes, Integrations and Security links. GPT’s header links form a uniform single-line row.

  2. W2 · Xiaomi MiMo

    同时出现的两个 Request a demo 按钮内边距不一致，导航内的按钮文字过于贴近边缘。

    查看依据与原始评价

    观察属性：padding · 原值：8px 2px · 已提交移植

    The two simultaneously visible Request a demo buttons have inconsistent internal spacing. The button inside the navigation list has almost no horizontal inset, making its label appear cramped against the pill edge compared with the separate header CTA. GPT uses a consistent padded treatment for its repeated primary demo buttons.

    MiMo’s left orange header pill places its two-line label close to the left edge; the right orange pill gives the same label noticeably more breathing room. GPT’s header and hero demo buttons share an even inset treatment.
