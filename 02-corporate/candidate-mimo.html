Our mission

Decarbonising power is a decision-making problem.

The technology to run a clean grid largely exists. What's missing is the confidence to act on it — minute by minute, project by project, budget by budget.

We founded Northstar Energy to close that gap. Our software turns messy, fragmented energy data into decisions that engineers, financiers and operators can defend: where to build, when to charge, what to curtail, and how much carbon each choice actually saves.

Our story

Started in a substation control room in 2018.

Our founders spent a decade building forecasting models that never left the lab — and watching operators make calls on gut instinct because the models couldn't be trusted under pressure.

Northstar Energy began as a two-person effort to fix that: put the model where the decision happens, show the uncertainty honestly, and earn trust one dispatch cycle at a time. Today we're a team of 74 across three continents, still guided by the same rule — never show a number we can't explain.

  1. 2018

    First forecast shipped

    A 72-hour regional forecast piloted with a single municipal utility in the Pacific Northwest.

  2. 2020

    Dispatch goes live

    Real-time battery optimisation enters production across 240 MW of storage assets.

  3. 2022

    Independent verification

    Our emissions methodology is reviewed by a third-party climate assurance panel.

  4. 2024

    Portfolio launches

    Scenario planning for developers and asset managers, built on the same trusted core.

Common questions

The things procurement teams ask us first.

How does Northstar handle our operational data?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, isolated per tenant, and processed in the region you choose. We are SOC 2 Type II audited and ISO 27001 certified, and we never train shared models on customer data without a written agreement. You can export or delete your data at any time.

How long does implementation take?

A typical Forecast deployment takes four to six weeks from kickoff to production, including data connection, back-testing and operator training. Dispatch deployments depend on control-system integration and usually run eight to twelve weeks. We publish a dated plan in week one.

Can we validate the models before committing?

Yes. Every engagement begins with a back-test against your historical data, scored on metrics your team defines. If the model does not beat your current baseline, we say so and you owe us nothing for the pilot.

Does Northstar replace our existing systems?

Rarely. We integrate with SCADA, ADMS, historian, market and ERP systems through documented APIs and file-based connectors. Northstar sits alongside your stack as the analytical and optimisation layer, and your teams keep their systems of record.

How is pricing structured?

Annual subscriptions based on the modules you use and the capacity under management, with no per-seat charges. Pilot pricing is fixed-fee and credited toward your first year if you convert. We'll give you a written quote after the first call.
