Our mission

Make clean energy the default for every industrial operation.

We believe the path to a stable climate runs through the facilities that power our economy. Northstar Energy exists to give operators the clarity and control they need to cut waste, lower emissions, and build resilience — without compromising output.

Our team combines deep expertise in energy systems, industrial software, and field operations. We work alongside customers to turn ambitious climate goals into measurable, everyday progress.

Our story

Built by operators, for operators.

Northstar Energy began in 2016 when a small team of energy engineers and software developers set out to solve a problem they knew firsthand: industrial facilities were generating vast amounts of data but lacked the tools to act on it.

Today we work with manufacturers, food processors, and logistics providers across North America and Europe. Our platform is designed to meet the realities of industrial environments — rugged, complex, and always moving.

2016 Founded by energy engineers and software developers.
2019 Launched Northstar Signal for real-time monitoring.
2022 Added Dispatch and Ledger to complete the platform.
2025 Designed for industrial operations across sectors.
