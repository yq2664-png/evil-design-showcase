Our mission

Make clean energy the default, everywhere the grid reaches.

We believe the energy transition is an engineering problem and a human one — and it deserves tools built with warmth, clarity, and rigor.

Northstar was founded by grid operators and climate scientists who were tired of watching clean capacity go to waste because software couldn’t see it. Our platform helps operators forecast carbon intensity, orchestrate flexible demand, and dispatch storage at the moments it matters most.

Today, our systems coordinate more than 1.8 GW of distributed assets across three continents — from municipal microgrids in the Pacific Northwest to industrial campuses in the Ruhr Valley.

We are a certified B Corporation, a signatory to the Climate Pledge, and a proud partner of the Open Energy Data Alliance.

Our story

Built by operators, for operators.

Northstar began in a basement office in Bellingham, Washington, when two grid engineers and a climate scientist asked a simple question: why does the cleanest energy often arrive when no one is ready to use it?

Eight years later, we’re a team of 140 engineers, data scientists, and policy specialists working across Seattle, Berlin, and Melbourne. We remain independent, employee-owned, and stubbornly focused on the practical work of decarbonization.

  1. 2017

    Founded in Bellingham, WA, with a pilot at a regional utility.

  2. 2019

    Launched Northstar Signal, our first commercial carbon-forecasting product.

  3. 2021

    Opened European headquarters in Berlin; expanded into industrial demand response.

  4. 2023

    Northstar Ledger achieves third-party assurance for emissions reporting.

  5. 2025

    Crossed 1.8 GW of coordinated assets and became a certified B Corporation.
